  To further understand the limitations of hypothesis gener-
ation with high groundedness scores, we conducted an in-
depth analysis of the error patterns. We identified two repre-
sentative types of errors: (1) cases where the LLM incorrectly
infers that there is no association between the given entities,
despite supporting evidence; and (2) cases where the model
simply echoes or paraphrases the provided context without
engaging in substantive reasoning or hypothesis formation.
These findings highlight the need for more robust mecha-
nisms to ensure both accurate association detection and gen-
uine reasoning in hypothesis generation, enhancing the inter-
pretability and trustworthiness of the overall system [Doshi-
Velez and Kim, 2017; Loh et al., 2022; Miller, 2023; Sinha et
al., 2024a,b].
