E.2  Relational Privacy and Multi-Party Contexts


A deployment may enforce three relational privacy rules:
(1) Isolation: claim objects partition private statements from
shared context; (2) Relational Consent: disclosing pj’s state-
ments to pi requires authorization from pj; and (3) Asym-
metric Revocation: admitted consent changes update later
projections without rewriting historical state.
